Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ValutaSnap, an Android camera price scanner, travel currency converter, and shopping tally application, handles information. For privacy or support questions, email Valutasnap@gmail.com.

In brief: Price recognition happens on your device. Your saved photos, scans, shopping plans, purchases and trip archives are stored locally in the app, rather than uploaded to a ValutaSnap cloud account. We use Google ML Kit for text recognition and request currency reference rates from Frankfurter; those services have their own data-handling practices, described below.

1. Camera and scanned prices

With your permission, ValutaSnap accesses your device camera to recognise prices on price tags, menus and receipts. You can capture images, zoom in, verify prices and choose whether to save images, scanned amounts, or purchases. Text recognition is performed on the device using Google ML Kit; the receipt images and recognised text are not intentionally sent to a ValutaSnap server or to Google for text recognition.

2. Data saved on your device

The app locally stores information you choose to save, such as captured receipt or price-tag images, recognised and corrected prices, currencies, saved conversions and exchange-rate snapshots, shopping plans and budgets, purchase dates, labels and notes, named trip archives, and preferences such as handedness or selected currencies. This information is stored in app-private device storage. No ValutaSnap user account or cloud synchronization service is provided in this version.

3. Optional approximate location

ValutaSnap can request approximate device location to tag saved photographs with an approximate capture location. When permission is granted and a recent location estimate is available, approximate coordinates may be saved locally with the photo. Location tagging is optional: declining permission does not prevent scanning or saving pictures. The current app does not publish location pins to a remote map or send your saved coordinates to a ValutaSnap server.

4. Exchange-rate requests

To retrieve daily currency reference rates, ValutaSnap connects over HTTPS to the Frankfurter currency API. Currency-pair requests, device/network connection metadata such as IP address, and normal server request information may be visible to the API provider or network infrastructure. We do not intentionally attach your saved receipt images, purchase lists or exact location coordinates to these requests. Previously retrieved rates may remain available from local cache. Rates are reference values and may differ from the rate your card issuer or merchant charges.

5. Google ML Kit diagnostics

ValutaSnap uses Google's on-device ML Kit text-recognition SDKs. Google documents that ML Kit may collect device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance and error metrics, and feature-use or configuration information for diagnostics and usage analytics. This information is transmitted to Google over an encrypted connection. The bundled text-recognition SDK is designed to process image input on the device, rather than sending image contents to Google's servers for recognition.

For details, see Google's ML Kit data disclosure information and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Advertising, payments and analytics

As of the effective date, the current ValutaSnap release does not include an advertising network, in-app purchase or subscription service, third-party app analytics SDK, or developer-operated account tracking. This does not exclude the ML Kit diagnostics described above. If such features are introduced, this policy and the app's Google Play Data safety information will be updated as needed.

7. Retention, deletion and choices

  • You can review and correct stored prices in the app and delete selected saved images or scan records.
  • Deleting an image does not necessarily delete a purchase already saved in your Tally; it may instead remove the purchase's association with that image.
  • Archiving groups existing local records and is not cloud backup. Restoring an archive brings its records back into the active views.
  • Information stored on your device remains there until deleted in the app, cleared through Android app-storage settings, or removed by uninstalling the app, subject to device behavior.
  • Camera and location permissions can be changed from Android's app settings.

For information processed independently by Google ML Kit or the exchange-rate service, refer to those providers' applicable policies and retention practices.

8. Security

Saved images and other records use app-private Android storage, and network requests for currency data use HTTPS. No storage or transmission method is completely risk-free. Please avoid scanning or saving unnecessary sensitive payment-card numbers or other personal information.

9. Children

ValutaSnap is a general travel and shopping utility, not specifically designed for children. We do not intentionally provide a child-directed social network, advertising program or account system. Relevant Google Play target-audience and family requirements will be reflected in the app's distribution settings.

10. Updates and contact

We may update this policy if ValutaSnap's features, third-party services or data practices change. The latest effective date will appear at the top of this page. For support or privacy questions, contact Valutasnap@gmail.com.
